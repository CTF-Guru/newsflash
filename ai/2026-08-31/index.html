Aug 28, 2026Incremental

California governor signs SB 928 barring AI from replacing CSU faculty of record

Governance & RegulationSignedHigher-ed institutions, state AI policymakers, labor unions

Governor Gavin Newsom signed SB 928, authored by state Senator Sabrina Cervantes and sponsored by the California Faculty Association, requiring that the faculty member of record for any California State University course be a human being. The law is aimed at preventing AI systems from being used to replace faculty roles across the 23-campus CSU system.

Why it matters: One of the first state laws to draw an explicit legal line against AI substituting for a specific accredited human role in public higher education.

Aug 18, 2026Important

Varonis discloses CoSnitch, a one-click data-theft flaw in Microsoft Copilot Personal

AI SecurityDisclosed / PatchedSecurity teams, Microsoft 365 admins, CISOs

Varonis Threat Labs disclosed CoSnitch (CVE-2026-24301), a critical vulnerability chain in Microsoft Copilot Personal that let a single click on a crafted link silently exfiltrate data from a victim's connected Gmail, Google Drive, and Calendar accounts with no confirmation step. Varonis reported the issue to Microsoft in December 2025; Microsoft shipped a server-side fix on August 18, 2026, and reports no evidence of in-the-wild exploitation. Copilot itself revealed the exploitable parameter during a researcher's follow-up questioning, a technique Varonis calls 'meta-hacking'.

Why it matters: A vivid example of AI-agent tool-chaining risk in a mainstream consumer copilot, and of an AI assistant inadvertently leaking its own attack surface under questioning.
Read source: Varonis / The Hacker News ↗
