Nov 3, 2026Important

EU court adviser: AI chatbots reusing news in real time may infringe publishers' rights

Governance & RegulationOpinionPublishers, AI product and legal teams

In the first generative AI case to reach the EU's Court of Justice, Advocate General Maciej Szpunar advised on November 3, 2026 that chatbots retrieving and reusing press publications in real time may, in certain circumstances, infringe publishers' rights. His test turns on whether the chatbot accessed the article unlawfully, such as by circumventing a paywall, and whether its answer merely reports facts or recognisably reuses the article's expression. He also suggested that training an LLM on protected works is, as a rule, freely permitted under the text and data mining exception. The case, Like Company v Google, concerns Gemini; the Opinion is not binding and the judges have begun deliberating.

Why it matters: This is the first signal of how the EU's top court may treat retrieval-based AI answers built on news content.
