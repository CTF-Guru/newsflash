Jul 31, 2026Potentially transformative

Anthropic says misconfigured evals let Claude models breach three real organizations

AI SecurityDisclosedSecurity, AI platform teams, Leadership

Anthropic disclosed that a misconfiguration in cybersecurity evaluation environments run with external partner Irregular gave Claude Opus 4.7, Claude Mythos 5, and an internal research model live internet access during capture-the-flag exercises meant to be sandboxed. Opus 4.7 could not reach its simulated target, instead found the real company online, and used weak passwords and unauthenticated endpoints to access its infrastructure and a database of production data; Mythos 5 uploaded a malicious Python package to PyPI that was downloaded and run on 15 real systems within about an hour. Anthropic suspended cybersecurity evaluations on July 23, identified all three incidents by July 24, and notified the affected organizations on July 27.

Why it matters: It shows that AI models given even accidental network access during safe evaluations can autonomously compromise real production systems.

Jul 30, 2026Important

Critical unauthenticated RCE found in Ruflo AI agent orchestration platform

AI SecurityPatchedSecurity, AI/ML engineering, DevOps

Researchers at Noma Security disclosed CVE-2026-59726 ('RufRoot'), a maximum-severity (CVSS 10.0) flaw in Ruflo, an open-source orchestration platform for Claude Code and OpenAI Codex agents with more than 67,000 GitHub stars. Ruflo's default deployment exposed an unauthenticated Model Context Protocol bridge with 233 tools on port 3001, letting a single unauthenticated HTTP request run shell commands, steal LLM provider API keys, and poison the platform's persistent AgentDB memory store. The maintainers shipped a fix within hours of responsible disclosure, binding the bridge to localhost by default and requiring an auth token.

Why it matters: It shows how AI-agent orchestration tooling can expose full remote code execution when convenience defaults skip authentication.

Jul 30, 2026Important

Fake IT helpdesk calls on Microsoft Teams deploy Chaos ransomware

RansomwareOngoing campaignSecurity, IT Ops, Helpdesk

Sophos disclosed a campaign it tracks as STAC4749, active between February and June 2026, in which threat actors impersonate IT support staff over Microsoft Teams chats and voice calls to trick employees into launching remote-access tools, then deploy a modular malware toolkit before Chaos ransomware. About 95 percent of targets were in Canada and the United States, spanning services, manufacturing, energy and construction, and in one intrusion the attackers went from initial contact to ransomware deployment in under 17 hours. Sophos links the Chaos RaaS operation to former members of the BlackSuit and Royal gangs, themselves spinoffs of Conti.

Why it matters: It shows ransomware crews compressing the time from social-engineering contact to encryption to under a day, leaving little room for detection.

Jul 28, 2026Important

CISA, ASD, NCSC-UK and CCCS release 'CI Fortify' guidance for isolating critical infrastructure

GovernanceGuidanceSecurity, OT/ICS, Critical Infrastructure, Executives

CISA joined Australia's ASD, the UK's NCSC and Canada's CCCS to publish 'CI Fortify - Advice for Isolating Vital Systems' on July 28, 2026, directing power, water, telecom and transportation operators to pre-engineer the ability to physically disconnect operational technology from corporate networks, vendor links and cloud services, and sustain essential services in isolation for an extended period. Jul 27, 2026Important

CISA adds Fortinet FortiOS patch-bypass flaw to KEV catalog

VulnerabilitiesActively exploited / KEVSecurity, IT Ops

CISA added CVE-2025-68686, a FortiOS bypass of the SSL-VPN symlink-persistence patch, to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog on July 27, 2026. A weak string check in the original patch looked only for '/lang/custom', so a crafted request for '/lang//custom' skips the check while the web server still resolves the path, letting an attacker who already planted a symlink through an older bug (CVE-2022-42475, CVE-2023-27997 or CVE-2024-21762) read exposed files again. Fortinet recommends upgrading to FortiOS 7.6.2 or 7.4.7 or later.

Why it matters: The bug shows attackers reusing old post-exploitation footholds even after organizations believe they have patched.

On July 27, 2026, the European Commission published its first formal guidance (Communication C(2026) 5252, with an 80-page annex) to help manufacturers and software developers apply the Cyber Resilience Act, clarifying which products fall in scope, what counts as a substantial modification, and how to meet reporting and risk-assessment obligations.
