Oct 21, 2026Important

AI-assisted research uncovers critical 'PHP2Shell' remote code execution flaws in PHP

VulnerabilitiesPatchedPHP site operators, web hosting and AppSec teams

PHP released 8.5.12, 8.4.27, 8.3.36 and 8.2.35 on October 21, 2026 to fix two critical flaws, CVE-2026-109618 and CVE-2026-109619, that can allow unauthenticated remote code execution on PHP installations serving web requests, including PHP-FPM, Apache with mod_php, php-cgi, FrankenPHP and LiteSpeed. Wiz Research, working with Calif and OpenAI, says it used Wiz Atlas, Gemini 4 Argon and GPT-5.6 Sol to find and exploit them; CNN reports government sites in France, Denmark, Finland and the Netherlands went temporarily offline while patching. On October 23 PHP also advised setting session.upload_progress.enabled=0 until its next release.

Why it matters: Frontier models turning hard-to-exploit bugs in core web infrastructure into working exploits shrinks the window defenders have to patch.

Oct 21, 2026Important

Malicious LiteLLM 1.107.0 release on PyPI and Docker shipped a credential stealer

AI SecurityRelease pulledPython and AI developers, platform and supply-chain security teams

On October 21, 2026, attackers published a malicious release of LiteLLM, a popular Python proxy library for LLM providers with about 23 million weekly downloads. Version 1.107.0 was live on PyPI and in LiteLLM's official Docker images for about two hours, installing a credential stealer that targets cloud, developer, AI-tool, browser and LiteLLM proxy credentials, plus a second-stage remote access trojan. LiteLLM says the attackers used stolen GitHub CLI OAuth tokens from three engineers' accounts to launch its private release workflow; it is the library's second supply-chain compromise this year.

Why it matters: One poisoned release of a library that sits between apps and every model provider can harvest exactly the API keys and cloud credentials AI teams hold most.

Oct 16, 2026Important

Anthropic privately pitches Congress a federal frontier-AI safety bill

GovernanceReported / draft circulatingAI policy teams, frontier AI developers, Congress watchers

Politico reported on October 16, 2026 that Anthropic has been privately circulating a draft federal frontier-AI bill reflecting its Advanced AI Framework. Government could halt a model only for a foreseeable, imminent threat of more than $1 billion in damages or the deaths or serious injuries of over 50 people, limited to bioweapons, models escaping their developers' controls, or cyberattacks - and Commerce would need a federal judge's order to suspend a model, with independent evaluators overseeing compliance. The draft would also preempt some state rules on risk disclosures, independent evaluations and incident reporting; an Anthropic spokesperson called them 'sensible policies we believe Congress should act on.'

Why it matters: A leading AI lab writing its own proposed incident-reporting and model-suspension framework for Congress is a notable escalation in frontier-AI cyber governance.

VulnerabilitiesActively exploitedWindows, Office and .NET administrators; vulnerability-management teams
