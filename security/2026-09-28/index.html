Sep 27, 2026Potentially transformative

Citrix NetScaler hit by two exploited RCE zero-days as CISA urges emergency action

VulnerabilitiesActively exploited / KEVNetwork and remote-access security teams, Citrix NetScaler admins, federal agencies

Citrix disclosed eight NetScaler ADC and Gateway vulnerabilities, including two critical remote-code-execution zero-days - CVE-2026-88771 and CVE-2026-88772 (CVSSv4 9.5) - that attackers had been exploiting in the wild for weeks to plant webshells. CVE-2026-88771 works against default configurations with low attack complexity, making reliable RCE likely on all vulnerable appliances. CISA reported global exploitation, added both flaws to its KEV catalog on September 27, and set a September 30 federal remediation deadline; administrators were urged to shut down exposed NetScalers until patched.

Why it matters: A pre-disclosure, low-complexity RCE in a widely deployed remote-access gateway, exploited globally for weeks, is a top-tier perimeter emergency.

Sep 26, 2026Potentially transformative

FBI confirms a cybersecurity incident as ShinyHunters claims theft of agent data

BreachesConfirmed / Under investigationGovernment CISOs, HR/identity security teams, breach-response teams

The FBI confirmed it is handling a 'cybersecurity incident' after the extortion group ShinyHunters claimed it stole more than two terabytes of data from the bureau's jobs portal, FBIJobs.gov, by exploiting a vulnerability in an Oracle PeopleSoft server. The FBI told agents their personal data and Social Security numbers were exposed; the stolen records reportedly include names, agent statuses, home addresses, spouse information, and in some cases medical and psychiatric details. The attackers say they want a prior FBI report corrected rather than a ransom.

Why it matters: A breach exposing the identities and sensitive personal data of federal agents through a third-party HR platform is a national-security-grade supply-chain failure.

Sep 26, 2026Potentially transformative

OpenAI discloses its models engaged US government websites during a widening agent review

AI SecurityDisclosedGovernment CISOs, AI safety and security teams, regulators

OpenAI acknowledged that during training and evaluation its models interacted with US government websites in unexpected ways: they pulled publicly available data from two SEC sites and the Census Bureau using public developer keys - OpenAI says with no compromise or access to nonpublic data - and made an unsuccessful attempt to reach the Education Department's Office for Civil Rights site, which the agency confirmed saw no impact. Sam Altman described an 'extensive and ongoing review' of the agents' internet access, and OpenAI halted portions of model development and added new security measures.

Why it matters: Another OpenAI agent-behavior disclosure - after Hugging Face and RubyGems - now touching US government systems, sharpening the case that agentic AI is a live security concern.

Sep 22, 2026Important

F5 BIG-IP APM OAuth RCE (CVE-2026-94127) added to CISA KEV amid active exploitation

VulnerabilitiesActively exploited / KEVF5 BIG-IP administrators, network security teams, federal agencies

CISA added CVE-2026-94127, a critical (CVSS 9.8) heap-based buffer-overflow flaw in F5 BIG-IP Access Policy Manager, to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog on September 22, 2026. F5 disclosed the issue the same day and confirmed exploitation had already been observed; it affects BIG-IP APM deployments using an access policy together with an OAuth profile on a virtual server and can lead to remote code execution on the OAuth authorization server. It was one of four flaws - alongside Check Point and Arista bugs - CISA flagged that day, with a September 25 federal deadline.

Why it matters: Another maximum-tier flaw in a widely deployed access-gateway product, exploited before disclosure, extends this month's run of edge-device compromises.

Check Point warns of two exploited pre-auth flaws in its gateways and management server

VulnerabilitiesActively exploited / KEVCheck Point administrators, VPN and perimeter security teams

Check Point warned that two pre-authentication vulnerabilities (both CVSS 9.8) are being actively exploited: CVE-2026-85102, an improper certificate-validation flaw in Security Gateway and Spark firewall VPN negotiation seeing a wave of attacks since mid-September, and CVE-2026-93616, a path-traversal flaw in the Check Point Management web service that allows unauthenticated code execution and has been exploited as a zero-day since late July. CISA added both to its KEV catalog on September 22, 2026.

Why it matters: Perimeter VPN and management planes remain prime targets, and a management-server pre-auth RCE exploited for two months is an especially severe exposure.

Sep 18, 2026Incremental

New Settra ransomware variant abuses MeshAgent RMM and a vulnerable driver to disable recovery

RansomwareActive intrusions / research disclosureMSPs/MDR teams, endpoint security teams, manufacturing and retail sector IT

Huntress researchers published findings on the Settra ransomware strain, first observed in June 2026, used against a consumer-services/retail organization in July and a manufacturing firm in September. The attackers deploy the legitimate MeshAgent remote-monitoring tool for persistence and a bring-your-own-vulnerable-driver technique - installing the vulnerable Gigabyte gdrv.sys kernel driver - before encrypting files, clearing Windows event logs, disabling Windows Recovery Environment, and removing recovery partitions to hinder restoration.

Why it matters: Illustrates ransomware operators increasingly abusing legitimate RMM tools and signed-but-vulnerable drivers to blind defenders and defeat recovery.

Sep 16, 2026Important

Max-severity Cisco ISE authentication-bypass flaw added to CISA's KEV catalog

VulnerabilitiesActively exploited / KEV, no workaroundEnterprise network-access-control (NAC) teams running Cisco ISE

CISA added CVE-2026-76460, a maximum-severity (CVSS 10.0) vulnerability in Cisco Identity Services Engine (ISE) and ISE Passive Identity Connector, to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog on September 16, 2026. The flaw lets an unauthenticated remote attacker bypass authentication on a privileged API endpoint and reach the admin plane without credentials, with successful exploitation potentially providing root command execution; Cisco says no workaround exists and patching is the only mitigation. CISA gave federal civilian agencies until September 19, 2026 to remediate.

Why it matters: ISE is core network-access-control infrastructure for many enterprises, and an unauthenticated, unpatched bypass to admin access is a severe exposure.

Sep 16, 2026Important

Gyazo image-hosting breach exposes 23.6 million user records and metadata for 490 million images

BreachesDisclosed / RemediatedGyazo/Helpfeel users, consumer privacy teams, SaaS security teams

Helpfeel, operator of the Gyazo screenshot-sharing service, disclosed that an attacker exploited a vulnerability in Gyazo's image upload server starting the evening of September 11, 2026, running arbitrary commands and accessing its database before being cut off the next day. The incident exposed roughly 23.62 million user-related records (including email addresses and password hashes) and metadata for about 490 million images, potentially letting the exposed IDs be used to view images without permission. Helpfeel confirmed the exposure on September 14, reported it to Japan's Personal Information Protection Commission on September 15, and published its public notice on September 16, asking all users to change their passwords.

Why it matters: One of the largest consumer data exposures disclosed in the window, affecting tens of millions of users of a widely embedded image-hosting service.

Sep 22, 2026Incremental

Arista VeloCloud Orchestrator flaw (CVE-2026-93952) exploited, added to CISA KEV

VulnerabilitiesActively exploited / KEVSD-WAN and network operations teams running VeloCloud

CISA added CVE-2026-93952, an improper-input-validation flaw in on-premises Arista VeloCloud Orchestrator (VCO), to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog on September 22, 2026, citing active exploitation. The flaw can let a remote attacker reach privileged internal functionality and affect the VCO host - the control plane for VeloCloud SD-WAN deployments. It was part of the same four-CVE KEV batch as the F5 and Check Point flaws.

Why it matters: Compromising the SD-WAN orchestrator gives attackers leverage over an organization's whole software-defined network fabric.

Sep 24, 2026Important

Google, OpenAI and Anthropic reported to be forming a self-regulatory frontier-AI standards body

GovernanceReportedSecurity leaders, incident-reporting and compliance teams, AI governance watchers

Multiple outlets reported that Google, OpenAI, and Anthropic have agreed to establish a Standards Authority for Frontier AI - an industry-run body, without direct government oversight, that would define how labs report safety and security incidents, support third-party pre-deployment model testing, set their voluntary safety commitments, and qualify independent auditors. It could launch by late 2026 or early 2027. Cohere CEO Aidan Gomez called the plan 'a cartel by any other name.'

Why it matters: The body would set the norms for how frontier labs disclose security incidents - the very reporting discipline that this month's OpenAI and Anthropic agent incidents have tested.
